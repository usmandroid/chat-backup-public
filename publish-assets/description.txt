ChatGPT Backup is a simple browser extension that lets you save your ChatGPT conversations as Markdown or HTML files with a single click.

Features:
- Save entire conversations in Markdown or HTML format
- One-click download from the ChatGPT interface
- Works on both Chrome and Firefox
- No data is sent outside your browser; everything is saved locally

Why install?
- Keep a personal archive of your AI chats
- Share formatted conversations easily
- Backup important information for future reference

This extension is open source and privacy-friendly. No account or signup required.
